Great Ape Habitat Loss and Protected Area Effectiveness

How much forest has been lost across great ape habitat, and is forest loss lower within protected areas?

Featured by Orangutan Foundation International Canada ↗

I combined 25 years of satellite-derived forest loss data with IUCN great ape ranges and protected-area boundaries across 13 countries in Africa and Southeast Asia.

The analysis compares forest loss inside and outside protected areas, with country-level maps, annual trends and protected-area coverage presented through an interactive Quarto analysis.

Read analysis ↗ View code ↗

Key finding

Forest loss was 63% lower inside protected areas in the African study area and 73% lower in Southeast Asia.

Data

Hansen Global Forest Change, IUCN species ranges and WDPA protected-area boundaries.

Methods

Raster analysis, spatial intersection, time-series aggregation and interactive visualization.

Tools

Python (rasterio, geoandas, pandas, plotly), QGIS and Quarto.

September 2026

Borneo Fire Detection Tracker

Where are recent satellite-detected fires occurring in relation to Bornean orangutan habitat?

Developed with Orangutan Foundation International Canada ↗

I developed a monitoring tool to explore where recent satellite- detected fires overlap with Bornean orangutan habitat.

The tool combines NASA FIRMS fire detections with orangutan range data, allowing users to explore the location and characteristics of recent detections. Automated updates keep the map current without manual processing.

Open live map ↗ View code ↗

Use case

Quickly identify recent fires occurring within Bornean orangutan habitat.

Data

NASA FIRMS VIIRS active-fire detections and IUCN Bornean orangutan range data.

Pipeline

API retrieval, confidence filtering, spatial intersection and automated publishing.

Tools

Python (geopandas, pandas), Folium and GitHub Actions.

Multilateral Fund Project Portfolio Dashboard

How can the Multilateral Fund's global environmental project portfolio be explored through a single interactive interface?

I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard for exploring Multilateral Fund-supported environmental projects across more than 140 countries.

The dashboard combines project approvals, disbursements, ozone-depleting substances phased out and associated climate benefits with geographic, country and sector-level filtering.

Purpose

Making global environmental funding and project outcomes easier to explore and compare across countries and sectors.

Focus

About

My background combines environmental science, public policy and applied data work. I studied environmental science at the University of Ottawa and public policy at Concordia University, and currently work at the UNEP Multilateral Fund Secretariat in Montreal.

I'm interested in using data to investigate environmental questions, understand patterns of change and examine the outcomes of conservation efforts. My independent projects bring together publicly available datasets, analytical methods and visualizations to explore these questions.

I enjoy the process of moving from a research question to evidence that can be interpreted and communicated clearly. I'm particularly interested in work that combines research, analysis and communication to support better decisions.
